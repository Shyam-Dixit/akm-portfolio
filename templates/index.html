About

The Officer · IRSME
Ashok Kumar Misra
General Manager (Retd.) · Western Railway
Thirty-seven years on the line

A member of the Special Class Railway Apprentice (SCRA) 1983 batch, Ashok Kumar Misra joined at Jamalpur in April 1984. His batch is equivalent to the 1986 batch of the Engineering Service. His railway postings took him to the steam locomotives of Dahod Workshop in 1990 and, in a turn of poetic symmetry, later to overseeing the creation of a ₹20,000-crore electric locomotive factory in that very town.

Across eleven cities, five railway zones and RDSO, he served as Divisional Railway Manager of Jhansi, Principal Chief Mechanical Engineer of East Central Railway, Additional General Manager of North Eastern Railway, and ultimately General Manager of Western Railway, leading more than 1,100 officers and a workforce of nearly 95,000 across six divisions, from the headquarters at Churchgate, Mumbai.

An engineer by training and an administrator by temperament, he retired on 30 June 2025. To mark his commitment to fitness and discipline, he bicycled 27 km on the morning of his retirement day.
